Who We Are

Bakersfield's Trusted Cleaning Crew

What started as a one-truck operation has grown into Bakersfield's most trusted mobile car wash and pressure washing service. We're not a franchise — we're your neighbors, and we treat every property like it's our own.

Over the years we've washed thousands of vehicles, driveways, homes, and commercial properties across Kern County. Every job gets the same attention to detail, the same quality products, and the same commitment to leaving you completely satisfied.
