Why It Matters

More Than Just Clean — It Protects Your Home

Regular pressure washing isn't just about looks. Mold, mildew, algae, and dirt build-up can break down paint, stucco, and concrete over time — costing you in repairs. Annual cleaning extends the life of every surface it touches.

  • → Removes mold & mildew before it causes damage
  • → Extends the lifespan of paint and sealant
  • → Improves curb appeal and property value
  • → Safer surfaces — removes slippery algae and moss
  • → Prepares surfaces for repainting or resealing
  • → Removes oil stains that standard hoses can't touch
